Hello everyone, long time no see!

I got real damn close to releasing on iOS, I really did. There were just a few
measly steps left to get it on the app store. Somewhere along the way, I lost
the time and motivation to continue. I've been travelling for about a month and
have now got myself in a situation where the MacBook I bought for iOS
development is in use on the other side of the country. 

I don't have the same amount of time and energy as I did when
getting this started and, in retrospect, part of me was afraid of success. If
Directory did start snowballing in popularity, I don't know
if I'd have time to keep up with it.

The good news is that I've decided not to shut Directory down. I will keep the
Android and web versions running, perhaps even with some infrequent updates. 

And if a **knight in shining iOS armor** decides to join my efforts and manage iOS
testing and releases, the project has a good chance to continue. But for now
I'm taking a step back. It's been fun, and amazing how far the project
came in such a short time.

Thanks for following up to this point and if any readers have Apple friends
who would be interested in tinkering, do tell them to reach out and check the
description on the [support page](donate.md).

All the best

~ Cecilia
